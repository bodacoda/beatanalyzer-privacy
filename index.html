2. No collection or storage of data

BeatAnalyzer has been designed so that no personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted to us or to third parties. In particular:

  • No registration and no user account is required.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising services are used.
  • The app itself does not send any crash reports or usage statistics.
  • No data is stored on our own servers or with cloud providers.
  • No data is passed on to third parties.

3. Audio features

BeatAnalyzer records audio signals through the microphone in order to analyze the tempo (BPM) and the musical key.

All analyses are performed exclusively on your device and are only started at your instruction. The results are processed only temporarily in memory, shown on screen and discarded when the app is closed. They are neither stored permanently nor transmitted to us. We do not operate any servers through which requests are routed.

4. App Store

When you download the app through an app store (e.g. the Apple App Store), data is processed by the respective operator. We have no influence on this processing. The privacy policy of the respective provider applies, such as Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Contact

If you contact us by email, we use your details exclusively to process your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them as soon as they are no longer needed for that purpose and no statutory retention obligations apply.

6. Your rights

Because BeatAnalyzer does not store any personal data, we generally hold no data about you. Regardless of this, you have the following rights under the GDPR:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Lodging a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR)

7. Children

BeatAnalyzer does not knowingly collect data from persons of any age, including children under 16.

8. Changes to this privacy policy

We will update this privacy policy whenever the features of the app or the legal requirements change. The version published on this page applies in each case.
